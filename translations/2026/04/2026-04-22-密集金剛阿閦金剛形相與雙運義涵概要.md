## 【正文】

Guhyasamaja（密集金剛），特別是其阿閦金剛（Akṣobhyavajra）形相，是一位重要的無上瑜伽密續本尊，與其空行伴侶（明妃）雙運（Yab-Yum，union）而住，其明妃通常為觸金剛（Sparśavajra）。他被描繪為深藍色身，具三面（白、藍、赤）六臂，於雙手主臂持金剛（vajra）與金剛鈴，以表悲心與智慧之雙運。

### 密集金剛雙運相之重點特徵

**1. 形象（Iconography）**  
二尊常皆示現為三面六臂，莊嚴種種璎珞飾物，結金剛跏趺坐（vajra posture）。

**2. 持物與手印（Attributes）**  
於雙運相擁之中，密集金剛之雙手主臂交叉結金剛吽迦羅印（vajra-huṃkāra-mudrā），手中持金剛（vajra）與金剛鈴。其餘諸手多持法輪、蓮花、寶劍與如意寶珠。

**3. 意義（Meaning）**  
此雙運相，象徵智慧（明妃）與悲心或方便（密集金剛）之不二本性，顯示究竟成佛之境界。

Guhyasamaja, specifically in his Akshobhyavajra form, is a major Highest Yoga Tantra deity shown in yab-yum (union) with his consort, usually Sparshavajra. He is depicted as dark blue, with three faces (white, blue, red) and six arms, holding a vajra and bell in his primary hands to symbolize the union of compassion and wisdom.     
Key Aspects of Guhyasamaja in Yab-Yum:    
Iconography: Both deities are often depicted with three faces and six arms, adorned with ornaments and seated in a vajra posture.    
Attributes: In their embrace, Guhyasamaja's hands are crossed in vajra-humkara-mudra (holding a vajra and bell). Other hands typically hold a wheel, lotus, sword, and gem.    
Meaning: This union symbolizes the inseparable nature of wisdom (consort) and compassion or skillful means (Guhyasamaja), representing the ultimate enlightened state.
