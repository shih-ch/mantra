## 大圓滿的透空虹光：清淨的見（淨相）與不淨顯現的能量來源

## 【正文】

大圓滿心髓的根本基（gzhi），是一切清淨的見與不淨顯現的根本來源，不可思議，完全超越一切概念性的理解。

它透過本具的三種強大能力來解說：本初清淨的體性、光明澄澈而能了知的自性，以及無間斷的悲心能量。體性、自性與悲心能量具足三種特徵性的能量，稱為光輝、遊舞與妙力（gdang rol pa rtsal gsum）。

這三種能量細微的內在開展，源自根本基（gzhi）與覺性的自然光輝（rig pa'i gdangs），更具體地說，源自悲心能量的自然光輝（thugs rje'i gdangs），因此依光輝、遊舞、妙力的次第排列。

另有一些解說，從表現能量（妙力）（rtsal）的顯現面向切入，因而依妙力、遊舞、光輝的次第排列；表現能量的顯現，是不可思議諸顯現（無論清淨或不淨）向外生起的最後過程。

前一種內在開展以鏡中清晰映現各種形像為喻，說明輪迴與涅槃諸顯現的生起。後一種則將這些顯現描述為增飾的莊嚴或美化的飾物（rgyan），與「光明燦然的內在與外在遊舞」（rol pa）密切相關。

因此，在這組三種特徵性能量之外，本具功德的鮮明現前與顯然現起（例如佛身與本初智慧（sku dang ye shes）），以及看似外在的輪迴與涅槃之顯現，都被視為珍貴的飾物，美化並莊嚴各自的境域。

以下出自龍欽巴《甚深精髓》的簡要導引，似乎在某種程度上銜接了這兩種進路。它以如下方式提出「增飾的莊嚴」（rgyan）一詞，並一併提出兩種「光明燦然的內在遊舞」（rol pa）：

「關於生起的方式，〔一切清淨的見與不淨顯現〕都從悲心能量的光明燦然的內在遊舞（thugs rje'i rol pa）中生起、顯現。

簡要點出生起方式的要義：有表現能量（妙力）（rtsal）、增飾的莊嚴（rgyan）與光明燦然的內在遊舞（rol pa）三個面向。它們安住於自身的內在界域，也就是根本基的「任運圓滿珍寶空藏」（lhun grub rin po che'i sbubs gzhi），以及該根本基的光明顯現（dang gzhi snang）之中。當它們生起為外在的光明澄澈時，顯現的方式就如同各種形像映現於鏡中。

至於悲心能量的光明燦然的內在遊舞（rol pa）如何安住於自身的內在界域：覺性的體性雖然是空，卻具有無礙的生起管道，也就是它無間斷的表現能量（妙力）（rtsal）。

由於它〔本初清淨而〕空的體性不落入空無一物的斷滅狀態，本初智慧的增飾莊嚴（ye shes kyi rgyan）就不會淪為僅僅是任運圓滿的能知覺照。它無實質、無阻礙之通透性的增飾莊嚴，也就是它的自然光輝（rang gdangs），不落入空白、物質性的空；因此它透過覺性的增飾莊嚴，真實地現前為精妙莊嚴者（rig pas rgyan pas rgyan yod pa）。

〔同樣地，〕悲心能量的光明燦然的內在遊舞，包含覺性本具自然光輝（rig pa rang gdangs）的光明燦然的內在遊舞，以及該光輝作為本初智慧的光明燦然的內在遊舞（rol pa），後者從那個狀態向外生起為光明澄澈。因此，〔覺性自然光輝的〕光明燦然的內在遊舞，是透過〔本初智慧的〕光明燦然的內在遊舞而現前的；所以，它是遊舞之中的遊舞。

此外，這三個面向安住為根本基唯一而獨特的覺性狀態，因此實際上是不二的。證悟這三者時，稱之為『功德』，因為正是由於覺性本身的偉大，它們才以如此方式顯現。」

南開囊久導讀並翻譯，Yephel（待考），2026 年 9 月 20 日。願此文能帶來啟發與助益！

生起方式即是悲心的遊舞。以三種生起方式簡要開示其義：表現能量（妙力）、增飾的莊嚴、光明燦然的內在遊舞這三者，存在於內在法界的任運成就珍寶空藏根本基與基顯之中，向外生起為光明，如同形貌映現於鏡中。

若問它們在內在法界中如何存在：覺性的體性雖然是空，生起的門徑卻無阻滯，所以有妙力。由於不落入斷空，本初智慧的莊嚴不落為任運成就的有覺者；通透無實的莊嚴，也就是自光輝，不落為頑空；以覺性為嚴飾，所以有莊嚴。

覺性遊舞為自光輝，光輝遊舞為本初智慧，由此向外生起為光明，所以有遊舞之遊舞。這三者同為一個根本基覺性，因此在義理上無二。證悟這三者時，稱為功德，因為覺性本身的偉大就是這樣顯現的。

## 【疑點/勘誤】

- 位置：英文引文「(dang gzhi snang)」  
  疑點：藏文原句作「སྦུབས་གཞི་དང་གཞི་སྣང」，其中「dang」是連詞「與」，不是「光輝」（gdangs）。  
  建議：讀為「gzhi dang gzhi snang」（根本基與基顯）。英文譯作「luminous appearances」，可能誤把 dang 當成 gdangs。  
- 位置：「(gdang rol pa rtsal gsum)」  
  疑點：「gdang」拼寫不完整。  
  建議：作「gdangs rol pa rtsal gsum」，與文中「rig pa'i gdangs」「rang gdangs」一致。  
- 位置：「(rig pas rgyan pas rgyan yod pa)」  
  疑點：藏文作「བརྒྱན་པས」。  
  建議：轉寫應作「rig pas brgyan pas rgyan yod pa」。brgyan 是動詞「嚴飾」，rgyan 是名詞「莊嚴」。  
- 位置：藏文「ནང་དབྱིངས་སུ་ཇེ་ལྟར་ཡོད་ན」  
  疑點：「ཇེ་ལྟར」拼寫有誤。  
  建議：作「ཇི་ལྟར」（如何）。  
- 位置：藏文「བྱེད་ཀྱི་རྣམ་པ་མེ་ལོང་དུ་ཤར་བ」  
  疑點：「བྱེད」（作者、能作）在此不合文意。  
  建議：作「བྱད་ཀྱི་རྣམ་པ」（形貌，待考），與英譯「distinct forms being reflected in a mirror」相合。  
- 位置：藏文末句「སྣང་བའི་ཕིར་རོ」  
  疑點：「ཕིར」缺下加字。  
  建議：作「ཕྱིར」（因為）。

Dzogchen Thögal – Energetic Sources for Pure Visions and Impure Appearances     
Regarding the basic ground of Dzogchen’s Heart Essence, which abides as the fundamental source of all pure visions and impure appearances, inconceivable and completely beyond all kinds of conceptual understanding, it is explained through its three inherent powerful capacities - its primordially pure essence, its luminously clear and cognizant nature, and its unceasing compassionate energy. Essence, nature, and compassionate energy are endowed with three characteristic kinds of energies known as dang rolpa and tsal (gdang rol pa rtsal gsum).     
As the subtle internal evolvement of these three energies originates from the basic ground (gzhi) and Awareness’s natural radiance (rig pa’i gdangs) and more specifically from its compassionate energy’s natural radiance (thugs rje’i gdangs) they follow the order of dang, rolpa, and tsal. Instead, other explanations starting from the aspect of the manifestation of the expressive energy (rtsal), which is the final process of the external arising of inconceivable appearances – pure as well as impure - follow the order of tsal, rolpa, and dang. While the former internal involvement uses the example how distinct forms clearly appear as the reflections in a mirror for the arising appearances of both saṃsāra and nirvāṇa the latter describes those manifestations as embellishing adornments or beautifying ornament (rgyan), which are closely related to the 'luminously radiant internal and external display' (rol pa). Thus, additional to the triad of three characteristic kinds of energies the vivid presence and manifest appearance of inherent qualities such as the enlightened bodies and primordial wisdoms (sku dang ye shes) as well as the seemingly external manifestations of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa are regarded as precious ornaments, which beautify and embellish the respective spheres.     
The following concise introduction from Longchenpa’s Zabmo Yangtig seems to somehow bridge these two approaches by introducing the term 'embellishing adornment' (rgyan) together with two kinds of the 'luminously radiant internal display' (rol pa) in the following manner:    
“Regarding the mode of arising [all kinds of pure visions and impure appearances] arise and appear from compassionate energy’s luminously radiant internal display (thugs rje'i rol pa).     
To briefly highlight the essential meaning of the mode of arising there are the three aspects of the expressive energy (rtsal), the embellishing adornment (rgyan), and the luminously radiant internal display (rol pa), which abide within one’s internal dimension, the 'spontaneously perfect jewel-like empty enclosure' of the basic ground (lhun grub rin po che'i sbubs gzhi), and the luminous appearances of that basic ground (dang gzhi snang). When they arise as external luminous clarity they appear in the same manner as distinct forms being reflected in a mirror.     
As for the manner how compassionate energy’s luminously radiant internal display (rol pa) abides within one’s internal dimension even though the essence of Awareness is empty it possesses an unobstructed avenue of arising as its unceasing expressive energy (rtsal).     
Because its [primordially pure and] empty essence does not lapse into a void state of nothingness, the embellishing adornment of primordial wisdom (ye shes kyi rgyan) is not reduced to a mere cognizant awareness of spontaneous perfection. And as the embellishing adornment of its insubstantial, unimpeded transparency - its natural radiance (rang gdangs) - does not lapse into a blank, material emptiness, it is truly present as being exquisitely ornamented through the embellishing adornment of Awareness (rig pas rgyan pas rgyan yod pa).     
[In the same manner, as compassionate energy’s] luminously radiant internal display consists in the luminously radiant internal display of Awareness’s inherent natural radiance (rig pa rang gdangs) and the luminously radiant internal display (rol pa) of that radiance as primordial wisdom, which externally arises as luminous clarity from that state, the luminously radiant internal display [of Awareness’s natural radiance] is present through [primordial wisdom’s] luminously radiant internal display; thus, it is the internal display within that internal display.    
Furthermore, since these three aspects abide as the basic ground’s single and unique state of Awareness, they are actually non-dual. When these three are realized, they are called 'virtuous qualities’, for it is through the greatness of Awareness itself that they appear in such a way.”
