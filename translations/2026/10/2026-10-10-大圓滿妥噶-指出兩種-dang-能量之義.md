# 大圓滿的透空虹光（Thögal，穿越法）：指出兩種「Dang」能量之義

## 【正文】

### 第一段

> According to the Heart Essence of the Great Perfection (rdzogs chen snying thig) there are several subtle energies used to describe the mode of arising and the manifestation of the universe and its beings as well as dzogchen thögal's visionary appearances leading to the attainment of the rainbow body.

依大圓滿心髓（rdzogs chen snying thig）之說，有若干微細能量，用以描述宇宙及其眾生的生起方式與顯現，以及導向證得虹身的大圓滿透空虹光（Thögal，穿越法）之種種淨相顯現。

### 第二段

> While the potential or capacity of one's Natural State (gnas lugs) endowed with the three energies of the natural radiance (gdang), the luminously radiant internal display (rol pa), and the expressive energy (rtsal) and their successive subtle internal evolvement became more widely known, the aspect of the external manifestation via the three aspects of the expressive energy (rtsal), the embellishing adornment (rgyan), and the luminously radiant internal display (rol pa), which abide within one's internal dimension, are rarely explained in more detail. In this respect, even though Awareness's natural radiance (rig pa'i gdangs) is likewise regarded as the source of all kinds of manifestations it is not emphasized. Moreover, its essential origin, often designated as natural self-radiance of the basic ground (gzhi'i rang gdangs) is not even mentioned.

自身本然狀態（gnas lugs）具足三種能量：自然光輝（gdang）、明光燦然的內在遊舞（rol pa）與力用（rtsal）。此本然狀態的潛能或力用，及其相續的微細內在開展，已較廣為人知。然而，經由力用（rtsal）、莊嚴（rgyan）與明光燦然的內在遊舞（rol pa）三面向而成的外在顯現，則鮮少有較詳細的解說；此三面向安住於自身內在界域之中。就此而言，覺性的自然光輝（rig pa'i gdangs）雖同樣被視為一切種種顯現的來源，卻未受到重視。此外，其根本源頭，即常稱為「基之自然自光輝」（gzhi'i rang gdangs）者，甚至未被提及。

### 第三段

> Now, regarding the Tibetan term dang (gdangs) we find the spelling gdangs and mdangs, which many learned lamas and Western scholars regard simply as orthographic variants. Consequently, even certain editions of Longchenpa's famous works such as The Seven Treasures and its commentaries prefer one or the other orthography. Moreover, they state that "gdangs is vocal or audial impulse or dynamism and hence it could mean 'tune, melody, lyrics, etc. ' whereas mdangs is optical or visual impulse and hence is connected with 'colour, shimmer, glow, etc. ' Instead, David Germano points out "that Longchenpa strictly differentiates between 'glow' (mdangs) and 'radiation' (gdangs), with the former signifying a non-manifest internal glowing associated with 'internal radiance' (nang gsal), and the latter signifying externally manifest radiation into actualized forms associated with 'external radiance' (phyir gsal)." In this respect in Longchenpa's Zabmo Yangtig we find the following clear explanation:

關於藏文詞 dang（gdangs），文獻中可見 gdangs 與 mdangs 兩種拼寫。許多博學喇嘛與西方學者僅將二者視為拼寫異體。因此，即使龍欽巴名著如《七寶藏》及其注釋的某些版本，亦各自偏用其中一種拼寫。他們並表示：「gdangs 是聲音或聽覺上的衝動或動態，因此可指『曲調、旋律、歌詞等』；而 mdangs 是光學或視覺上的衝動，因此與『顏色、微光、光澤等』相關。」

與此不同，大衛·傑馬諾（David Germano）指出：「龍欽巴嚴格區分『光澤』（mdangs）與『放射』（gdangs）。前者意指與『內明』（nang gsal）相關、未顯現的內在光澤；後者意指與『外明』（phyir gsal）相關、向外顯現為已實現形相的放射。」就此而言，龍欽巴《甚深極精髓》（Zabmo Yangtig）中有如下清楚的解說：

### 龍欽巴《甚深極精髓》引文（英譯原文與繁中譯文）

> "Regarding the expression 'luminously clear pleasure of the lotus-like nature' (pad+ma bde gsal) this refers to one's internal dimension (nang dbyings), which is spontaneously perfect and naturally luminous by - and in itself. There are many who mistake [the essential nature of] the 'interior glow of penetrating transparency' (thal gyis mdangs) for [the capacity of its] 'natural radiance' (gdangs), or who fail to properly distinguish between the 'interior glow' (mdangs) and its 'natural radiance' (gdangs), such that all [the capacities of] the natural radiance is lost within a mere 'luster' (mdangs).

「關於『蓮花般本性之明晰樂』（pad+ma bde gsal）此一用語，是指自身的內在界域（nang dbyings），其本身任運圓滿、自然明晰。有許多人將『通徹透明之內在光澤』（thal gyis mdangs）〔的本質〕誤認為其『自然光輝』（gdangs）〔的力用〕，或未能正確區分『內在光澤』（mdangs）與其『自然光輝』（gdangs），以致自然光輝〔的一切力用〕全數失落於僅僅一種『光澤』（mdangs）之中。

> From now onwards, you have to distinguish between them: 'interior glow' refers to the internal luminosity (nang 'od) and its lucid appearance represented by a blue colored [sphere, which abides] within the natural self-clarity of its own essential nature, whereas 'natural radiance' refers to the aspect of the external luminous clarity (phyir gsal) [arising] as [concentric circles of] five luminous lights and likewise extremely subtle appearances resembling genuinely pure tiny sparks and fine threads.

從今以後，你必須區分二者：『內在光澤』是指內在光明（nang 'od）及其明晰顯現，其表徵為〔安住於〕自身本質之自然自明中的藍色〔明點〕；而『自然光輝』是指外明（phyir gsal）的面向，〔生起〕為五光〔的同心圓〕，以及同樣極為微細、宛如真正清淨之細小火花與細絲的顯現。

> Thus, while the 'interior glow' is to be understood as the internal luminous clarity of the internal dimension (dbyings nang gsal), 'natural radiance' must be comprehended as the external luminous clarity (phyir gsal). Moreover, 'interior glow' is to be understood as being inseparable from its essence (ngo bo dang dbyer med par mdangs), while 'natural radiance' must be comprehended as what arises from that essence (de las shar ba gdangs). Therefore, one should clearly differentiate one from the other."

因此，『內在光澤』應理解為內在界域之內明（dbyings nang gsal），『自然光輝』則須理解為外明（phyir gsal）。此外，『內在光澤』應理解為與其本質無別（ngo bo dang dbyer med par mdangs），『自然光輝』則須理解為從該本質所生起者（de las shar ba gdangs）。故應清楚區分二者。」

Dzogchen Thögal – Pointing Out the Meaning of the Two Dang Energies    
According to the Heart Essence of the Great Perfection (rdzogs chen snying thig) there are several subtle energies used to describe the mode of arising and the manifestation of the universe and its beings as well as dzogchen thögal’s visionary appearances leading to the attainment of the rainbow body.     
While the potential or capacity of one’s Natural State (gnas lugs) endowed with the three energies of the natural radiance (gdang), the luminously radiant internal display (rol pa), and the expressive energy (rtsal) and their successive subtle internal evolvement became more widely known, the aspect of the external manifestation via the three aspects of the expressive energy (rtsal), the embellishing adornment (rgyan), and the luminously radiant internal display (rol pa), which abide within one’s internal dimension, are rarely explained in more detail. In this respect, even though Awareness’s natural radiance (rig pa'i gdangs) is likewise regarded as the source of all kinds of manifestations it is not emphasized. Moreover, its essential origin, often designated as natural self-radiance of the basic ground (gzhi'i rang gdangs) is not even mentioned.    
Now, regarding the Tibetan term dang (gdangs) we find the spelling gdangs and mdangs, which many learned lamas and Western scholars regard simply as orthographic variants. Consequently, even certain editions of Longchenpa’s famous works such as The Seven Treasures and its commentaries prefer one or the other orthography. Moreover, they state that “gdangs is vocal or audial impulse or dynamism and hence it could mean 'tune, melody, lyrics, etc. ' whereas mdangs is optical or visual impulse and hence is connected with 'colour, shimmer, glow, etc. ' Instead, David Germano points out “that Longchenpa strictly differentiates between 'glow' (mdangs) and 'radiation' (gdangs), with the former signifying a non-manifest internal glowing associated with 'internal radiance' (nang gsal), and the latter signifying externally manifest radiation into actualized forms associated with 'external radiance' (phyir gsal).” In this respect in Longchenpa’s Zabmo Yangtig we find the following clear explanation:      
“Regarding the expression 'luminously clear pleasure of the lotus-like nature' (pad+ma bde gsal) this refers to one’s internal dimension (nang dbyings), which is spontaneously perfect and naturally luminous by - and in itself. There are many who mistake [the essential nature of] the 'interior glow of penetrating transparency' (thal gyis mdangs) for [the capacity of its] 'natural radiance' (gdangs), or who fail to properly distinguish between the 'interior glow' (mdangs) and its 'natural radiance' (gdangs), such that all [the capacities of] the natural radiance is lost within a mere 'luster' (mdangs).    
From now onwards, you have to distinguish between them: 'interior glow' refers to the internal luminosity (nang 'od) and its lucid appearance represented by a blue colored [sphere, which abides] within the natural self-clarity of its own essential nature, whereas 'natural radiance' refers to the aspect of the external luminous clarity (phyir gsal) [arising] as [concentric circles of] five luminous lights and likewise extremely subtle appearances resembling genuinely pure tiny sparks and fine threads.    
Thus, while the 'interior glow' is to be understood as the internal luminous clarity of the internal dimension (dbyings nang gsal), 'natural radiance' must be comprehended as the external luminous clarity (phyir gsal). Moreover, 'interior glow' is to be understood as being inseparable from its essence (ngo bo dang dbyer med par mdangs), while 'natural radiance' must be comprehended as what arises from that essence (de las shar ba gdangs). Therefore, one should clearly differentiate one from the other.”
