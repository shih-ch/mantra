# 大圓滿的透空虹光（Thögal，穿越法）：「對境」如何經由覺性本然光輝之鮮明展現而顯現為莊嚴飾

---

## 【正文】

### 英文本文

清淨的見（淨相）（dag snang）與不清淨的業力見，二者現象顯現的實際生起，被視為自身內在創造力的外在展現，也就是覺性（rig pa）的表現能量（自心覺性之能量，rig pa'i rtsal）從其內在向度中生起。作為自身本然潛能（自己潛能，rang rtsal）的外在「投射」，一切感官顯現或者顯現為自心的概念對境，或者顯現為覺性的莊嚴飾（rgyan）。龍欽巴（klong chen pa）在《甚深精髓》（Zabmo Yangthig）中，針對整個宇宙及其有情的顯現，以及大圓滿（rdzogs chen）透空虹光（穿越法）修持中的境相顯現，將此過程總結如下：

「至於［輪迴（'khor ba）之不淨顯現與涅槃（mya ngan las 'das pa）之清淨的見的］實際生起方式與顯現：從覺性的表現能量（rig pa'i rtsal）生起五種本初智慧（ādijñāna / ye shes）。從其表現能量［ye shes lnga'i rtsal］，即五色光明之光芒（'od zer lngar），生起［五種微細］元素。從其表現能量［'byung ba lnga'i rtsal］，生起［五種粗重］元素，作為外在器世間的物質形態。從其表現能量［'byung gzugs lnga'i rtsal］，生起花朵等種種［植物］。

從［五種］光明明點（thig le 'od lnga）［自然放射］的五色光明，生起身體的五種［粗、細］元素及其表現能量［lus kyi 'byung lnga rtsal］。由此生起蘊、界、處等（phung po khams dang skye mched la sogs / skandha dhātu āyatana）。

再者，藉由證悟［覺性的表現能量］，涅槃的表現能量生起為五身與五種本初智慧，連同其相關的利生事業。因此，其功德以無止息、無障礙的方式自然自顯。」

他主要依據《大普明明點續》（thig le kun gsal chen po'i rgyud），詳盡闡釋這些顯現乃是莊嚴飾或美化之飾（rgyan）。這些莊嚴飾與「光明放射之內外遊舞」（rol pa）密切相關，是從自身覺性的表現能量（rig pa'i rtsal）所生起的顯現。其說明如下：

「關於『表現能量』（rtsal）的全面理解，有五個主題：（1）體性（ngo bo / svabhāva）；（2）釋名（nges tshig / nirukti）；（3）分類（dbye ba / prabheda）；（4）表示之喻（mtshon dpe / dṛṣṭānta）；（5）生起方式（'char tshul）。

1. 『表現能量』的體性，被確認為無止息、無障礙（ma 'gags pa）。
2. 其釋名：因具有顯現之力，故稱為『表現能量』。
3. 其分類有二：迷亂感知顯現為輪迴，以及證悟顯現為涅槃。因此有輪迴與涅槃兩類。
4. 其喻為：一個太陽顯現為眾多太陽；太陽無止息的光芒；烏頭（aconite）之毒是否被咒（mantra）所化解；樟腦；或如閃電之一閃。
5. ［關於悲心之光輝（thugs rje'i gdangs）的生起方式：］空性覺性如同［透明的］水晶球，僅作為內在光明得以生起的潛能或能力而存在。在此空性覺性之中，五種本初智慧之光輝自然自顯（ye shes lnga'i rang gdangs），其本然表現能量（自己潛能，rang rtsal）顯現為五種光明。從此本然表現能量，諸身與智慧的光明顯現作為外在光明而生起。

若未如實證悟其真實面目，輪迴二取執著的表現能量便自然生起為迷惑的迷亂顯現。若已證悟，涅槃之本初智慧的表現能量便生起為二種色身，即報身（saṃbhogakāya）與化身（nirmāṇakāya）的清淨境相顯現。

第二，［關於『莊嚴飾』（rgyan）的全面理解，有五個主題：（1）體性（ngo bo / svabhāva）；（2）釋名（nges tshig / nirukti）；（3）分類（dbye ba / prabheda）；（4）喻（dpe / dṛṣṭānta）；（5）表示詞（mtshon tshig / lakṣaṇapada）：

1. 『美化之飾』或『莊嚴飾』的體性，是以差別功德美化本質功德。
2. 其釋名：凡所存在者，皆因其存在而得美化，故稱為『莊嚴飾』。
3. 分類有三種：（1）三身是法性（dharmatā）的『莊嚴飾』；（2）二色身是法身（dharmakāya）的『莊嚴飾』；（3）五光，即本初智慧的本然自光輝，是前二者的『莊嚴飾』。
4. 喻有三：（1）空性法性之境以法身為莊嚴，如同虛空以太陽為莊嚴；（2）五身之法身以［五種］本初智慧之覺性為莊嚴，如同五色孔雀蛋；（3）五種本初智慧以覺性為莊嚴，喻如孔雀雛鳥。

   或者，亦可依次理解為：鑲嵌綠松石的黃金；放射［五種］光明的水晶；以及［完全成形的大鵬金翅鳥］雛鳥［在蛋中］，孵化破殼而出時［即已能飛翔］。
5. 關於表示詞，［《大普明明點續》（thig le kun gsal chen po'i rgyud）］亦如是說：「在我所宣說的一切密續（tantra）與傳承中皆說：『法身安住於法性之境中。當其光輝被描述為五智時，即解說為法身以［五］智為莊嚴。』五智之五光圓滿為諸身的方式，以及其各自部族、顏色與形狀的描述，則解說為『智慧之主』以悲心能量為莊嚴。」

第三，［關於『光明放射之內在遊舞』（rol pa）的全面理解，有五個主題：（1）體性（ngo bo / svabhāva）；（2）釋名（nges tshig / nirukti）；（3）分類（dbye ba / prabheda）；（4）表示之喻（mtshon dpe / dṛṣṭānta）；（5）證悟方式（rtogs tshul）：

1. 『光明放射之內在遊舞』的體性是圓滿殊勝，具足其現象存在（chos，法）之功德，並以隨後於［現象存在的本然自性（chos nyid，法性）］而顯現為特徵。
2. 其釋名：它是從自身生起者的內在遊舞，亦即光明放射之內在遊舞，故稱為『光明放射之內在遊舞』。因此，它是該內在遊舞之中的內在遊舞。
3. 關於［二種］分類：
   （1）法性清淨的光明放射之內在遊舞。法性清淨，即現象存在之本然自性的［本初］清淨狀態。此遊舞從覺性之光輝（rig pa'i gdangs）生起為諸身、智慧與悲心能量。
   （2）法性不清淨的外在遊舞。法性不清淨，即現象存在之本然自性的不清淨狀態。此遊舞生起為器世間及其有情之迷亂感知的顯現。

   因此，色（視覺顯現）是佛身的外在遊舞；聲（聽覺顯現）是佛語的外在遊舞；概念憶念（正念之覺照與覺知）是佛意的外在遊舞。這些從佛身、語、意之光輝（sku gsung thugs gyi gdangs）生起，並作為［自身之身、語、意（lus ngag yid）］三門的外在遊舞而現前。
4. 關於［二種表示之］喻，［《大普明明點續》（thig le kun gsal chen po'i rgyud）］亦如是說：（1）海洋浪沫飛濺，又落回並消融於海洋；（2）幻術師所變現的種種形相之虛幻顯現。二喻闡明了無止息之光明放射［內外］遊舞的功德，此遊舞清晰地顯現為任何一切。『光明放射之遊舞』一詞雖有許多類似的喻，但此［二喻］總括了其［看似內在與外在的］放射功德。
5. 關於凡夫的證悟方式：迷惑的迷亂顯現自然自生，隨其任意顯現而被自然地執取，以致最終因［持續地］執其為真實，而停留於固著的狀態中。

   相對地，大圓滿瑜伽行者認出那些非實有的光明顯現僅是覺性的莊嚴飾，因而是覺性光明放射之外在遊舞［從覺性光明放射之內在遊舞中生起並顯現］。由此認出，那些幻化顯現便解脫為自然自顯的顯現。如此證悟其遠離有、無二邊，即是［證悟方式］。」

### 第一組：莊嚴之偈（藏文第 4 段）

1. །ངེས་ཚིག་དོན་གྱི་དབྱེ་བ་ནི།
   轉寫：nges tshig don gyi dbye ba ni
   漢音：額 次 頓 吉 耶 瓦 尼
   繁體中文翻譯：釋名之義的分別是：
   句義：引出下文，以偈頌說明三種莊嚴之義。

2. །ཆོས་ཉིད་དག་ལ་ཆོས་སྐུས་བརྒྱན༌།
   轉寫：chos nyid dag la chos skus brgyan
   漢音：確 尼 達 拉 確 估 堅
   繁體中文翻譯：清淨法性以法身為莊嚴，
   句義：法身是法性的莊嚴。

3. སྟོང་པ་ཕྱང་ཆད་གསལ་བས་མཛེས།
   轉寫：stong pa phyang chad gsal bas mdzes
   漢音：東 巴 槍 切 賽 威 則
   繁體中文翻譯：空性（phyang chad，待考）以明晰而為美；
   句義：空性之法性因明分而顯其莊嚴。

4. །ཆོས་ཀྱི་སྐུ་ལ་ཡེ་ཤེས་བརྒྱན།
   轉寫：chos kyi sku la ye shes brgyan
   漢音：確 吉 古 拉 耶 謝 堅
   繁體中文翻譯：法身以本初智慧為莊嚴，
   句義：本初智慧是法身的莊嚴。

5. ཡོན་ཏན་འགགས་པ་མེད་པས་མཛེས།
   轉寫：yon tan 'gags pa med pas mdzes
   漢音：雲 滇 嘎 巴 美 貝 則
   繁體中文翻譯：以功德無有止息而為美；
   句義：法身之莊嚴，表現為功德無礙不絕。

6. །ཡེ་ཤེས་དག་ལ་ཐུགས་རྗེས་བརྒྱད།
   轉寫：ye shes dag la thugs rjes brgyad
   漢音：耶 謝 達 拉 圖 傑 皆
   繁體中文翻譯：清淨本初智慧以悲心為莊嚴，（原文作 brgyad「八」，依上下文應為 brgyan「莊嚴」，見【疑點/勘誤】）
   句義：悲心是本初智慧的莊嚴。

7. །འགྲོ་དྲུག་དོན་ལ་སྦྱོར་བས་མཛེས།
   轉寫：'gro drug don la sbyor bas mdzes
   漢音：卓 竹 頓 拉 交 威 則
   繁體中文翻譯：以致力於六道有情之利益而為美；
   句義：悲心之莊嚴，表現為利益六道有情（ṣaḍ-gati）。

8. །དེ་ཕྱིར་མཛེས་པའི་རྒྱན་ཤེས་བྱ།

### 第二組：遊舞之喻偈（藏文第 7 段）

1. རྒྱ་མཚོར་ཆུ་བྲན་འཕྲོ་ཞིང་འདུ་བ་ལྟར།

Dzogchen Thögal – How 'Objects' Manifest as Embellishing Ornaments via the Vivid Display of Rigpa’s Natural Radiance    
The actual manifestation of phenomenal appearances of both pure visions and impure karmic visions is considered to be the external display of one’s internal creative power, the expressive energy of Awareness (rig pa'i rtsal) arising from within its internal dimension. As an external 'projection' of one's natural potentiality (rang rtsal) all kinds of sensory appearances manifest either as conceptual objects of one’s mind or appear as Rigpa’s embellishing ornaments (rgyan). Focusing on the manifestation of the entire universe and its beings and the visionary appearances of dzogchen’s thögal practice Longchenpa’s Zabmo Yangthig summarized the process:    
“As for the actual manner of arising and the manifest appearance [of saṃsāra’s impure appearances and nirvāṇa’s pure visions] from Awareness’s expressive energy (rig pa'i rtsal) arise the five primordial wisdoms. From their expressive energies [ye shes lnga'i rtsal] - the five-colored rays of luminous light ('od zer lngar) – arise the [five subtle] elements. From their expressive energies ['byung ba lnga'i rtsal] arise the [five coarse] elements as material forms of the external container of the universe. From their expressive energies ['byung gzugs lnga'i rtsal] arise the variety of [plants such as] flowers and so forth.     
From the five-colored luminous lights [naturally radiating] from the [five] luminous spheres (thig le 'od lnga) arise the five [coarse and subtle] elements of the physical body together with their expressive energies [lus kyi 'byung lnga rtsal]. From those arise the aggregates, elements, sense bases, and so forth (phung po khams dang skye mched la sogs/skandha dhātu āyatana).     
Furthermore, by realizing [Awareness’s expressive energy] the expressive energies of nirvāṇa arise as the five enlightened bodies and the five primordial wisdoms together with their related enlightened activities. Consequently, their virtuous qualities naturally manifest by themselves in an unceasing and unobstructed manner.”    
Mainly based on the Tantra of the Great All-Illuminating Sphere (thig le kun gsal chen po'i rgyud) he thoroughly explains those manifestations being the embellishing adornments or beautifying ornaments (rgyan), which are closely related to the 'luminously radiant internal and external display' (rol pa), as the manifest appearances arising from the expressive energy of one's own Awareness (rig pa'i rtsal) in the following manner:      
“As for a comprehensive understanding of 'expressive energy' (rtsal) there are five topics: (1.) essential meaning (ngo bo/svabhāva), (2.) etymology (nges tshig/nirukti), (3.) classification (dbye ba/prabheda), (4.) illustrative example (mtshon dpe/dṛṣṭānta), and (5.) mode of arising ('char tshul).    
1. The essential meaning of 'expressive energy' is identified as being unceasing and unobstructed (ma 'gags pa).    
2. Its etymological definition is 'expressive energy' because it possesses the power of manifestation.    
3. As for its classification, there are two: the appearance of deluded perception as saṃsāra, and the appearance of realization as nirvāṇa. Thus, there are the two divisions of saṃsāra and nirvāṇa.    
4. The examples are: a single sun appearing as many suns; the unceasing rays of light from the sun; whether or not aconite is neutralized by mantra; camphor; or like a flash of lightning.    
5. [As for the mode of arising of the compassionate energy’s natural radiance (thugs rje'i gdangs)] from within the empty Awareness that is likened to a [transparent] sphere of a crystal ball - existing merely as the potential or capacity for internal luminosity to arise - the natural expressive energy (rang rtsal) of the five primordial wisdoms’s natural radiances manifest by themselves (ye shes lnga'i rang gdangs) as five luminous lights. From this natural expressive energy, the luminous appearances of the enlightened bodies and wisdoms arise as external luminosity.    
When these are not realized for what they actually are, the expressive energy of saṃsāra’s dualistic grasping arises by itself as bewildering delusory appearances. When they are realized, the expressive energy of the nirvāṇa’s primordial wisdoms arises as the pure visionary appearances of the two enlightened form bodies, i.e. saṃbhogakāya, and nirmāṇakāya.    
Second, [as for the comprehensive understanding of 'embellishing adornment' (rgyan) there are five topics: (1.) essential meaning (ngo bo/svabhāva), (2.) etymology (nges tshig/nirukti), (3.) classification (dbye ba/prabheda), (4.) example (dpe/dṛṣṭānta), and (5.) illustrative words (mtshon tshig/lakṣaṇapada):    
1. The essential meaning of 'beautifying ornament' or 'embellishing adornment' is the beautification of the essential qualities by means of distinctive qualities.     
2. As for its etymological definition, it is called an 'embellishing adornment' because whatever exists is made beautiful by its presence.     
3. When classified, there are three types, (1.) the three kāyas are the 'embellishing adornment' of dharmatā; (2.) the two rūpakāyas are the 'embellishing adornment' of the dharmakaya, (3) and the five lights, which are the natural self-radiance of primordial wisdom, are the 'embellishing adornment' of both.    
4. There are three examples, (1.) the empty dharmatā state adorned by the dharmakāya is like the sky adorned by the sun, (2.) the dharmakāya of the five kāyas is adorned by the [five] primordial wisdoms’ Awareness is like a five-colored peacock’s egg', and (3.) five primordial wisdoms being adorned by Awareness is likened to a peacock chick.     
Instead, it could be understood respectively as gold inlaid with turquoise, a crystal radiating [five] luminous lights, and [fully developed garuda] chick [within its egg] when hatching the egg and emerging from its shell [it is ready to fly].    
5. Regarding the illustrative words [the Tantra of the Great All-Illuminating Sphere (thig le kun gsal chen po'i rgyud)] states likewise: "In all the tantras and transmissions I have taught, it is said: 'The dharmakāya abides within the state of dharmatā. When its natural radiance is described as the five wisdoms, it is explained that the dharmakāya is adorned by the [five] wisdoms.' The manner in which the five lights of the five wisdoms are perfected as the kāyas, and the description of their respective families, colors, and shapes, is explained as the 'lord of wisdom' being adorned by compassionate energy."    
Third, [as for the comprehensive understanding of the 'luminously radiant internal display' (rol pa) there are five topics: (1.) essential meaning (ngo bo/svabhāva), (2.) etymology (nges tshig/nirukti), (3.) classification (dbye ba/prabheda), (4.) illustrative example (mtshon dpe/dṛṣṭānta), and (5.) mode of realization (rtogs tshul):    
1. The essential meaning of the 'luminously radiant internal display' is perfect excellence, being endowed with the qualities of its phenomenal existence (chos) and characterized by appearing subsequent to [the intrinsic nature of phenomenal existence (chos nyid)].    
2. As for etymological definition, it is called an 'luminously radiant internal display' because it is the internal display of that which arises from itself as the luminously radiant internal display; thus, it is the internal display within that internal display.    
3. As for the [twofold] classification, (1.) it is the luminously radiant internal display of dharmatā’s purity – the [primordially] pure state of the intrinsic nature of phenomenal existence - which arises from the natural radiance of awareness (rig pa'i gdangs) as the enlightened bodies, wisdoms, and compassionate energy, and (2.) it is the external display of the dharmatā’s impurity – the impure state of the intrinsic nature of phenomenal existence - which arises as the appearances of the deluded perception of the physical environment and its sentient beings. Thus, forms - visual appearances are the external display of the enlightened body, sounds - auditory appearances are the external display of the enlightened speech, and conceptual recollections – mindful presence and awareness is the external display of the enlightened mind. These arise from the radiance of the enlightened body, speech, and mind (sku gsung thugs gyi gdangs), and become present as the external display of the three doors [of one’s body speech and mind (lus ngag yid)].    
4. Regarding the [two illustrative] examples [the Tantra of the Great All-Illuminating Sphere (thig le kun gsal chen po'i rgyud)] states likewise: (1.) the ocean foam’s flung spray and its collapsing and dissolving back into the ocean, and (2.) the illusory appearances of a great variety of forms created by a magician, elucidate the virtuous qualities of the unceasing luminously radiant [internal and external] display, which clearly manifests as anything whatsoever. Even though there are many similar examples for the term 'luminously radiant display' these [two] summarize its [seemingly internal and external] radiant qualities.     
5. As for the mode of realization for ordinary beings, bewildering delusory appearances, which naturally arise by themselves, are naturally grasped just as they randomly appear so that they eventually remain in a lasting condition by [continuously] clinging to them as being real.     
Instead, for the dzogchen yogic practitioner, by recognizing that those non-existent luminously clear appearances are just Awareness’s embellishing ornament and therefore its luminously radiant external display [which arises and appears from Awareness’s luminously radiant internal display,] those illusionary manifestations are liberated as natural appearances manifesting by itself. Thus, realizing them as being free from the extremes of existence and non-existence is [the mode of realization]."
