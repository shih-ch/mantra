### 二、金剛瑜伽空行之形相描述（出自 Khyabdag Nesar Ngawang Kunga Legpa’i Jungne）

1. 「……金剛瑜伽空行之形相：安住於多色蓮華與日輪座上，右足伸展，踏壓紅色迦羅羅緹（Kalaratri）之胸部，左足踏住黑色毗梨伽婆（Bhairava）向後屈曲之頭部。

2. 身色紅如火，大放光耀，如劫盡之火，一面二臂，三眼觀視清淨虛空天（Khechara）之境。

3. 右手持印有金剛（vajra）之屈曲刀向下伸展，左手持盈滿血之頭骨缽（skullcup）於上舉，面向上仰而飲。

4. 左肩上有印有金剛之羯多伐杖（katvanga），繫掛小鼓（damaru）、鐸（bell）與三面垂掛之幡旗。

5. 黑髮成束披散垂至腰際，年少相，具大欲之豐滿雙乳，其狀能引生福樂。

6. 頂戴五個乾枯人頭骨之冠，並具五十顆人頭骨之頸飾，全身裸露，具足五種印，以毘盧遮那佛（Vairocana）為頂嚴，安住於清淨覺知之熊熊火焰中央。」

### 三、畫面中諸尊配置說明（出自 Jeff Watt）

1. **頂端中央**  
   頂端中央為本初佛金剛持（Vajradhara），身色青。

2. **右上方**  
   右上方為勇猛金剛法（Vira Vajradharma），身色紅。

3. **左側人物**  
   左側有一尊身著僧衣、戴黑帽，雙手持二朵花，其上承托金剛與鐸之形相者。  
   雖然此像因前額橫向髮際線而顯得似為男子，但極有可能是表現「桑頂多傑帕莫」（Samding Dorje Pagmo），此尊常被描繪為戴黑帽、穿僧衣，並具金剛與鐸之相。

4. **下方中央護法**  
   下方中央為護法天神「勝屍林主」（Shri Shmashana Adhipati），現為二具舞蹈之骷髏身相——依薩迦（Sakya）傳承，為那若巴金剛瑜伽空行之特別護法。

5. **觀者左側護法**  
   於觀者左側為吉祥天母「瑪佐爾嘉莫」（Shri Devi Magzor Gyalmo）。

6. **觀者右側護法**  
   右側則為博東傳承與博東寺院之特別世間護法——「扎西沃巴」（Tashi Wobar），身色紅，以岑（Tsen）戰士之相現身。

## 【疑點/勘誤】

1. **Kalaratri 之譯名**  
   - 疑點：可見譯名有「迦羅夜」「迦羅羅緹」等，此處暫以「迦羅羅緹」音譯。（待考）

2. **Shmashana 拼法**  
   - 疑點：應源自梵文 śmaśāna（屍林），此處寫作 Shmashana，屬轉寫差異。（待考）

3. **five mudras（五種印）**  
   - 疑點：是否特指「五骨飾」或更廣泛之五印莊嚴，原文未交代，故不加詮釋，暫直譯為「五種印」。（待考）

Vajrayogini according to the tradition of mahasiddha Naropa.

Video: HAR #57048

‘...the form of Vajrayogini, on a multi-coloured lotus and sun seat, with the right foot extended pressing down on the breasts of red Kalaratri, left pressing on the backward bent head of black Bhairava. With a body red in colour, brilliant, like the fire at an aeon’s end, one face, two hands, three eyes gazing at the pure [realm] of Khechara. The right hand holds a vajra marked curved knife extended downward, left, a blood filled skullcup held above with the face looking upward and drinking. On the left shoulder is a vajra marked katvanga [staff] with a damaru, bell and three hanging pennants. Black hair in strands extends to the waist, youthful and with large breasts of desire, in a mood generating bliss. Having a crown of five dry human skulls and a necklace of fifty; naked, adorned with five mudras, Vairocana as a crown, standing in the middle of a blazing fire of pristine awareness.’ (Written by Khyabdag Nesar Ngawang Kunga Legpa’i Jungne, 1704-1761).

At the top center is the primordial Buddha Vajradhara, blue in colour. At the upper right side is Vira Vajradharma, red in colour. At the left side is a figure wearing monastic robes, a black hat, and holding two flower blossoms supporting a vajra and bell. Although the figure appears to be male due to the horizontal hairline on the forehead, it is very possible that the figure is representing the Samding Dorje Pagmo who is often depicted with a black hat, monastic robes, along with vajra and bell attributes.

At the bottom center is the protector deity Shri Shmashana Adhipati appearing as two dancing skeletons - the special protectors for Vajrayogini of Naropa according to the Sakya Tradition. At the viewer’s left side is Shri Devi Magzor Gyalmo. On the right side is the special worldly protector deity for the Bodong Tradition and Bodong E monastery - Tashi Wobar, red in colour, appearing as a Tsen warrior.
