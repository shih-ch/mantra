## 【正文】

### 原標題

Scorpion Protective Charm  
蠍形護符

### 逐段翻譯

There are numerous scorpion protective charms warding off and protecting practitioners from malevolant beings, both human and non-human such as king spirits or gyalpo (rgyal po), who have the pwer to seduce the practitioner by honoring their experiences and realizations.  

有許多以蠍子形象為主的護符，用來驅遣並守護行者，遠離各種惡意存在，包括人類與非人，如王靈或嘉波（rgyal po），這些存在有能力以恭維、迎合行者的修持經驗與證悟，來誘惑行者。

Thus, you'll find those charms often outside of the retreat area or outside of the retreat cabin above te door for example. Some use it at their hoomes too.  

因此，人們常把這類護符懸掛在閉關區外圍，或閉關小屋門楣上方等處；也有一些人會在自己家中使用。
