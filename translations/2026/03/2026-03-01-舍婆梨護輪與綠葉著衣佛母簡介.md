## 【正文】

### 1. 藏文標題與簡介

རི་ཁྲོད་ལོ་མ་ཅན་འམ་ལྷ་མོ་ལོ་གྱོན་མ་གྱི་བསྲུང་འཁོར་དཔེ་རིས།  
(ri khrod lo ma can 'am lha mo lo gyon ma gyi bsrung 'khor dpe ris)

譯：  
「山林葉衣尊（舍婆梨）或綠葉著衣佛母之護輪圖像。」

---

Parṇaśabarī Kavacacakra - Protective Wheel From Infectious Diseases  

譯：  
「舍婆梨（parṇaśabarī）護輪——防護傳染病之輪。」

---

The great artist Tashi Mannox brushed Parṇaśabarī's seed syllable PAM in pure saffron surrounded by her mantra in red color, while the outer black circle has the mantra of dependent origination (OM YE DHARMA...) with an extended sickness and obstacle dispelling prayer of body, speech and mind.

譯：  
「偉大的藝術家塔希·曼諾克斯（Tashi Mannox），以純番紅花（kuṅkuma）繪出舍婆梨（parṇaśabarī）的種子字『PAM』，外圍繞以紅色書寫之本尊真言；而最外層黑色圓圈，則書寫緣起（pratītya-samutpāda）真言（『嗡 耶達瑪……』等）及延展之祈願文，用以遮止疾病與一切身、語言與心之障礙。」

---

Parṇaśabarī known in Tibetan as Ritröma Lomachen or as Lhamo Logyönma (ri khrod ma lo ma can/lha mo lo gyon ma), 'Powerful Forest-Hermit-Ḍākinī Attired in Green Leaves [of Medical Plants that are Remedies to All Illnesses and Pestilences]', is also classified as the twentieth Tārā of the twenty-one forms of Tārā in the Nyingma terma tradition of Rigdzin Jigme Lingpa, and is invoked to protect from diseases and epidemics.

譯：  
「舍婆梨（parṇaśabarī），藏語名為『日確瑪洛瑪堅』（ri khrod ma lo ma can）或『拉莫洛琼瑪』（lha mo lo gyon ma），意為『身披醫藥綠葉、具大威神力之林間苦行空行母（ḍākinī）』。在寧瑪派覺囊（待考）·日金‧吉美林巴（Rigdzin Jigme Lingpa）之伏藏傳承中，她被歸為度母（tārā）二十一尊形相中的第二十度母，為了防護疾病與疫癘而被祈請。」

---

It is said that Buddha Śākyamuni said that reciting the mantra of the 'Leaf-Clothed Forest-Hermit-Ḍākinī will protect sentient beings, and all good wishes prayed for will be realized. It can eliminate all diseases and destroy obstacles, especially the obstacles and pain of infectious diseases. When the Buddha chanted this mantra, it shook the pure lands of various Buddhas, so the blessing power is very great.

譯：  
「相傳釋迦牟尼（śākyamuni）佛曾說：誦念這位『葉衣林中苦行空行母（ḍākinī）』的真言，能護持一切眾生（sattva），所祈求之善願皆得成就。此真言能遮止一切疾病與障礙，尤能摧毀傳染性疾病之種種障難與痛苦。當佛陀誦此真言時，諸佛淨土皆為震動，故其加持（adhiṣṭhāna）與威神力極大。」

---

The mantra of Parṇaśabarī aka Ritröma Lomachen or Lhamo Logyönma is om pishatsi parna shawari sarwa dzora prashamanaye soha (om pi shA tsi par+Na sha wa rI sarba dz+wa ra pra sha ma nA ya swA hA), which is in the center of the protective charm; it is explained as follows:

譯：  
「舍婆梨（parṇaśabarī），亦即『日確瑪洛瑪堅』或『拉莫洛琼瑪』之真言為：『om pishatsi parna shawari sarwa dzora prashamanaye soha』（藏語音標作：om pi shA tsi par+Na sha wa rI sarba dz+wa ra pra sha ma nA ya swA hA）。此真言位於護輪中央，其義解如下：」

---

OM: sacred syllable that consists of three sounds A, U, M representing Buddha’s purified body, speech and mind, which bless and activate our body speech and mind, plant the seed of the three kayas (nirmanakaya, sambhogakaya and dharmakaya). Here in particular, the sounds are invoked to prevent, protect and liberate our body, speech and mind from the pestilence such as the coronavirus.

譯：  
「OM：此為聖音節，由 A、U、M 三音構成，表徵佛陀清淨之身、語言與心，能加持並啟發我們自己的身、語言與心，種下三身（化身、報身與法身）之種子。在此特別是祈請此三音，防止、守護並解脫我們的身、語言與心，遠離如新冠肺炎等疫癘。」

---

PISHATSI: a female divinity of great powers such as a dakini or yogini who can protect, prevent and liberate beings from all demonic spirits, negative and obstructing forces

譯：  
「PISHATSI：指具大威神力之女性（strī）尊，如空行母（ḍākinī）或瑜伽行者（yogin），能護念、防護並解脫一切有情（jantu; prajā; puruṣa），遠離諸羅剎（graha）、負面與障礙之勢力（forces）。」

---

PARNA: a leaf of a tree or a plant

譯：  
「PARNA：樹或草本植物之葉。」

---

SHAVARI a tribal lady of the forest who masters magic and healing using forest herbs and medicines

譯：  
「SHAVARI：指一位居於森林中的部族女子，善於運用林間草藥與醫藥（Medicine）等術，通達咒術與療癒。」

---

PARNASHAVARĪ: a powerful forest-hermit-dakini who attired in green leaves of medical plants that are remedies to all illnesses and pestilences

譯：  
「PARNASHAVARĪ：即身披能療癒一切疾病與疫癘之藥草綠葉，具大威神力之林間苦行空行母（ḍākinī）。」

---

SARVA: all, everything

譯：  
「SARVA：一切、所有。」

---

DZORA: illness or pestilence

譯：  
「DZORA：疾病或疫癘。」

---

PRASHAMANIYE: pacifier

譯：  
「PRASHAMANIYE：寂止、平息者。」

---

SARVA MARI PRASHAMANI: the Pacifier of all illnesses and pestilences

譯：  
「SARVA MARI PRASHAMANI：『一切疾病與疫癘之寂止者』。」

---

SOHA: to attain the siddhis or attainments, to fulfill the aspiration, to be established in the deity-state, the Parnashavarī-hood.

譯：  
「SOHA：意為成辦成就（siddhi），令所發之願（Aspiration）得以圓滿，並安住於本尊狀態，即安住於舍婆梨（parṇaśabarī）之佛母位。」

   轉寫（Wylie）：om pi shA tsi par+Na sha wa rI sarba dz+wa ra pra sha ma nA ya swA hA  
   漢音：嗡 皮夏茨 巴爾納 夏瓦日 薩爾瓦 卓拉 帕夏瑪那耶 梭哈（待考）  
   繁體中文翻譯：  
   「嗡　具大威力之葉衣林中空行母，寂止一切疾病與疫癘者　梭哈。」（待考：依上文義綜合意譯）  
   句義：  
   「稱念聖音嗡，祈請葉衣林中空行母，寂止一切病與疫癘，成辦諸願並獲本尊之成就。」

   漢音：嗡 皮夏茨 巴爾納 夏瓦日 薩爾瓦 卓拉 帕夏瑪那耶 梭哈（待考）  
   繁體中文翻譯：  
   「嗡　具大威力之葉衣林中空行母，寂止一切疾病與疫癘者　梭哈。」（同上）  
   句義：  
   「重申此真言，以祈請舍婆梨佛母護除瘟疫與一切障難。」
