## 【正文】

### 一、題目與概述

**Wheel of Time – Karmacakras of the Kālacakratantra, Laghukālacakratantrarāja and Vimalaprabhā**  
「時輪（Wheel of Time）」——《時輪密續》（Kālacakratantra）、《略本時輪王密續》（Laghukālacakratantrarāja）及《淨光》（Vimalaprabhā）所說的業輪（karmacakras）。

關於時輪（kālacakra）所說身內粗細脈輪（cakra）的主與次細微結構──身體之業輪（karmacakra）、以及大地（earth）、水（water）、火（fire）、風（wind）、空界（space）五大（dhātu）之水道（vāhinī，細脈 nāḍī），並與本初智慧（ye shes/jñāna）之微細水道相連，其間有精細描繪與引人入勝的種種細節與圖像。

### 二、六大輪與諸業輪

依據著名的《時輪密續》（Kālacakratantra），藏文名為「時輪」（dus kyi 'khor lo），於頭頂、前額、喉嚨、心、臍與祕密處，各有六大輪（cakra）。此外，除這六大輪外，另有若干其他輪，例如位於男根中央部位的金剛寶輪（vajra jewel cakra）。

又，《無染莊嚴》（Ornament of Stainless Light）──為《淨光》（Vimalaprabhā）的一部具有權威性的藏文廣釋（upadeśa），由克珠諾桑嘉措（Khedrup Norsang Gyatso）於十五世紀所造──其中說道：「在三百六十個關節處所（處所 pradeśa）中，每一處皆有一輪；凡有一輪之處，就有風（vāyu）的種種事業（活動），亦即個人之命氣（般那〔命氣〕／prāṇavāyu，藏：rlung）的作用。」

在此意義上，《淨光》（Vimalaprabhā）進一步說明：這些位於身體關節處的輪，主管行走、步行、奔跑、持物、作業等一切動作，此類輪即名為「事業輪」（activity cakras），梵語稱為 karmacakras，藏語則稱為「業之輪」（las kyi 'khor lo）。

### 三、關節與藏醫所說的業輪

依據藏醫典籍，如《四部醫典》（The Four Medical Tantras，rgyud bzhi）等所說，人體具十二大關節與三百六十或六十四小關節，常為治療關節病（關節病／pārśvadāha，藏語：grum bu）及改善活動能力所對治之處。一般而言，十二大關節為：兩肩、兩肘、兩腕、兩髖、兩膝與兩踝；在其他傳承系統中也同樣如此描繪，例如著名的《那若六法》（Six Yogas of Nāropa），藏語稱為「那若六法」（nA ra chos drug）。

若從人體結構中較小關節與細身（subtle body）之能量點來分類，在此處有運載命氣（般那〔命氣〕／prāṇavāyu，藏：rlung）與精要（thig le／śuklabodhicitta，白淨菩提心）的細脈交會。格魯派（Gelugpa）與覺囊派（Jonangpa）等若干傳承，即將此等部位納入其氣與拙火、壯行（tsalung trulkhor）的精進（energy／精進）訓練之中。

### 四、業輪、要點與身關節

因此，除上述主、副水道輪或脈輪（主輪、次輪：'khor lo chen, rtsa 'khor chen/cakra）之外，尚有與身體關節相關之十二大、六十四小水道輪。此等輪部分對應於生命要害、脆弱與敏感之能量點，梵語稱為 marma；藏語稱為細脈「要點」（‘essential points of one’s subtle channels’），即「涅」或「擦涅」（ne 或 tsane，gnas, rtsa gnas/marma）；依時輪（Kālacakra）傳承，則稱此等為「事業輪」（las kyi 'khor lo/karmacakras）。

由於此等要點與其細脈皆對應於身體各關節之處所（處所 pradeśa），於諸藏醫傳統中稱之為「身之節」（lükyi tsig，lus kyi tshigs/kāyaparvan）。

### 五、《淨光》所說關節與細脈數目

就時輪（Kālacakra）傳承而論，《淨光》（Vimalaprabhā）於第 67–68 頌解釋：「在身體兩臂之六關節與兩腿之六關節之中（肩、肘、腕、髖、膝、踝各二處），被分類為十二種，構成十二個事業輪，即十二大身節（lus kyi tshigs chen bcu gnyis）。於每一處中有三十條細脈（rtsa／nāḍī），於此十二大關節處，合計共有三百六十條細脈。」

再者，「於手與足之六十個關節（4 x 5 指／趾 x 3 = 60）中，有六十個〔小〕事業輪。此等各自具足大地、水、火、風、空界以及本初智慧（ye shes/jñāna）等界（bhūta）之細脈自性（nature）。故於每一關節皆具六條細脈（五大加上本初智慧），於此六十個指關節與趾關節之中，共有三百六十條細脈。」

如是，依時輪（Kālacakra）所說：十二大關節中有三百六十條細脈，加上各關節指、趾節處之三百六十條細脈，合計共有七百二十條細脈。另一方面，依《那若六法》（The Six Yogas of Nāropa）所說，十二大關節中有九十六條細脈（12 x 8 = 96），六十四小關節中有二百五十六條細脈（64 x 4 = 256），合計共有三百五十二條細脈（96 + 256 = 352）。【疑點】原文括號處為「96 + 252 = 352」，明顯與前述 256 不符，疑為排版或數字誤植。

### 六、「時」與「輪」：呼吸、命氣與外內時輪

由於《時輪密續》（Kālacakratantra）是以「時間」（迦羅／kāla）與「輪」（cakra）之觀念為核心，其「時之輪」一方面指諸行星之循環軌度，一方面又指人體中呼吸（Breath）與細微能量之週期。

就外在或外顯之世俗現實（conventional reality／saṃvṛti）而言，「時輪」指日、月、歲（years／saṃvatsara）之流轉（circulation／流轉 saṃkrānti; saṅkrānti），以及十二宮黃道之循環；就個別或內在層面而言，則指「命氣（rlung/prāṇavāyu）在身中細脈輪（lus kyi rtsa／nāḍī）內之流轉」，此又與十二支緣起（緣起／pratītya-samutpāda）以及特別是黃道十二宮之各相互關連。這些不同層面的循環彼此互相聯繫、互相對應。

### 七、右臂三大關節與黃道：第一幅畫

因此，在右臂的三大關節中：肩關節對應摩羯座（Capricorn），肘關節對應雙魚宮（Pisces），腕關節對應金牛座（Taurus）。詳見第一幅畫之配置。

第二幅畫則依《淨光》（Vimalaprabhā）而作，其描繪與列表略有不同。例如，右肘關節連結徹特拉月（Caitra，藏：nag pa）──此月即太陽離開雙魚宮而進入白羊座之時。就此意義而言，第二幅畫之題記中說：「右肩之三十條細脈，對應瑪伽月（Māgha，藏：mchu'i zla）之三十朔望日，在此期間，太陽離開摩羯座。肘關節之三十細脈，對應徹特拉月（Caitra，藏：nag pa'i zla）之朔望日，在此期間太陽離開雙魚宮。其餘諸大關節亦如是推展。」

### 八、左右臂與手指諸關節的星座分配

左臂三大關節中：肩關節對應寶瓶座（Aquarius），肘關節對應白羊座（Aries），腕關節對應雙子座（Gemini）。進一步來說，左手三列指節所對應之星座與左臂三大關節所對應者相同：第一列（內側一列）指節配寶瓶座，中列配白羊座，外側一列配雙子座。右手相應的分配則為摩羯座、雙魚宮與金牛座。

### 九、雙腿與足趾關節的星座、五大與本初智慧

左腿三大關節中：髖關節對應獅子座（Leo），膝關節對應天秤座（Libra），踝關節對應人馬座（Sagittarius）；左足三列趾節亦配同樣星座。右腿與足趾相應關節，則分別對應巨蟹座（Cancer）、室女座（Virgo）與天蠍座（Scorpio）。

於畫面中，順著手臂與腿長度而行之細脈以不同色彩繪出，用以示意其與大地、水、火、風、空界五大（elements）及本初智慧之關聯。

### 十、再次引用右臂關節與月分

右肩之三十條細脈，對應瑪伽月（Māgha，藏：mchu'i zla）之三十朔望日，在此期間太陽離開摩羯座。肘關節之三十細脈，對應徹特拉月（Caitra，藏：nag pa'i zla）之朔望日，在此期間太陽離開雙魚宮。其餘諸大關節皆依此次第而配屬。

### 十一、關於不祕密、轉化外道與方便善巧

**重要說明（Important Note）：**  
在《內在時輪密續：一個關於補特伽羅之佛教密續觀》（The Inner Kalacakratantra: A Buddhist Tantric View of the Individual）一書中，Vesna A. Wallace 特別指出：「時輪密續（Kālacakratantra）採納某些外道（non-Buddhist）思想，其動機之一，即在於轉化諸異端（heterodox groups）。這事實顯示：有關時輪（Kālacakra）世界觀之教法，並未向大眾祕而不宣；亦即，它們並非只供予已受灌頂之菁英、作為祕密（secret）教法而被嚴格守護。」

「更進一步說，時輪傳承對於明白、直陳其特定密續見解之偏好，乃是由於其公開宣示的轉化（conversionary）事業所致……佛教密續之轉化努力，於此密續中被合理化為一種方便善巧（skillful means／方便善巧 upāyakauśalya），藉此引導具有種種（Diverse）心性傾向之補特伽羅（individual／pudgala; puruṣa）趨向心靈成熟（spiritual maturation）。」

此外，密續亦警示：一方面不應執著於自身見解、轉成教條式固執；但另一方面，在「為了駁斥他教而熟悉其教說」之時，又必須謹慎，不要反而落在他說之影響之下。

Wheel of Time – Karmacakras of the Kālacakratantra, Laghukālacakratantrarāja and Vimalaprabhā    
Beautiful drawings and fascinating details concerning Kālacakra’s subtle anatomy of main and secondary cakras, the karmacakras of one’s physical body, and the nāḍīs of the five elements of earth, water, fire wind, and space, together with the subtle channel of primordial wisdom.    
According to the famous Kālacakratantra known in Tibetan as Wheel of Time, Dükyi Khorlo (dus kyi 'khor lo), there are six major cakras at the level of the crown of the head, forehead, throat, heart, navel, and the secret place. Additionally, to those six main cakras are several other cakras such as the cakra of the vajra jewel located at the central part of the penis. Moreover, the Ornament of Stainless Light, an authoritative Tibetan exposition of the Vimalaprabhā, composed in the fifteenth century by Khedrup Norsang Gyatso states that “at each of the locations of the three-hundred sixty joints is a cakra, and wherever there is a cakra there are the activities of the winds, i.e. one’s vital energies (rlung/prāṇavāyu).” In this respect the Vimalaprabhā clarifies that these cakras at the physical body’s joints are responsible for one’s activities of moving, walking, running, handling, working, and the like they are known as activity cakras, karmacakras in Sanskrit and wheels of activity, lekyi khorlo (las khyi ‘khor lo) in Tibetan.    
According to Tibetan medicine such as The Four Medical Tantras (rgyud bzhi), the physical body contains twelve major joints and three-hundred sixty or sixty-four minor joints, often treated to relieve arthritis (grum bu) and enhance mobility. Generally, the body’s twelve major joints are the two shoulders, two elbows, two wrists, two hips, two knees, and two ankles, which are likewise depicted according to other systems such as the famous Six Yogas of Naropa, Nāro Chödrug (nA ra chos drug). Referring to the anatomical classification of one’s physical body's smaller joints and one’s subtle body’s energetical points, where certain subtle channels transporting vital energies (rlung/prāṇavāyu) and vital essences (thig le/śuklabodhicitta) meet, several traditions such as the Gelugpa and Jonangpa include them in their energetical training of tsalung trulkhor.    
Thus, additional to those main and secondary channel wheels or cakras (‘khor lo chen, rtsa ‘khor chen/cakra) there are twelve major and sixty-four minor channel wheels of one’s physical joints, which partially correspond to vital, vulnerable, and sensitive energy points known in Sanskrit as marmas, in Tibetan as ‘essential points of one’s subtle channels’, ne or tsane (gnas, rtsa gnas/marma), and according to the Kālacakra tradition as activity cakras (las kyi ‘khor lo/karmacakras). As those essential points and their subtle channels correspond to the location of the joints of the body in the various Tibetan medicine traditions they are referred to as lükyi tsig (lus kyi tshigs/kāyaparvan).     
Regarding the Kālacakra tradition the Vimalaprabhā (verses 67-68) explains that “within each of the body’s six joints of the two arms and six joints of the two legs (two each at the shoulders, elbows wrists, hips, knees, and ankles), which are classified into twelve types and are comprised of twelve activity cakras, i.e. major bodily joints (lus kyi tshigs chen bcu gnyis), there are thirty subtle channels (rtsa/nāḍīs) altogether three-hundred and sixty subtle channels in the twelve major joints. Furthermore, there are sixty [minor] activity cakras in the sixty joints of one’s hands and feet (4 x 5 fingers/toes x 3 = 60), which “are of the nature of the subtle channels of the elements earth, water, fire, wind, space, and primordial wisdom (ye shes/jñāna), respectively. Thus, they are present in every single joint and consist of the six subtle channels (of the five elements and primordial wisdom), altogether three-hundred and sixty subtle channels in the sixty joints of the finger and toe knuckles.” Thus, according to the Kālacakra there are three-hundred and sixty subtle channels of the twelve major joints plus three-hundred and sixty subtle channels of the joint knuckles, altogether seven-hundred and twenty subtle channels, while according to of The Six Yogas of Nāropa there are ninety-six subtle channels in the twelve major joints (12 x 8 = 96) plus two-hundred and fifty-six subtle channels in the sixty-four minor joints (64 x 4 = 256) altogether three-hundred fifty-two subtle channels (96 + 252 = 352).     
As the Kālacakratantra revolves around the concept of time (kāla) and cycles or wheels (cakra), the wheels of time refer to the cycles of the planets and to the cycles of human breathing and subtle energies in the body. Regarding the outer or external aspect of conventional reality, the wheel of time refers to the passage of days, month, and years, as well as the cycles of the zodiac, while with regard to the individual or inner aspect, it refers to "the circulation of one’s vital energies (rlung/prāṇavāyu). within the wheels of the subtle channels in the body (lus kyi rtsa/nāḍī), which is linked with the twelve aspects of dependent origination and, in particular, to the twelve signs of the zodiac. These different cycles are interconnected and correspond to each other.    
Consequently, from the three major joints of the right arm the shoulder is associated with Capricorn, the elbow with Pisces and the wrist with Taurus; see first painting.     
The second painting following the Vimalaprabhā depicts and lists these a little differently. For example, the right elbow is associated with the month of Caitra (nag pa); this is the month during which the Sun leaves the sign of Pisces and enters Aries. In this respect the inscription of the second painting states, “the thirty subtle channels of the right shoulder are associated with the thirty lunar days of the month of Māgha (mchu'i zla), during which the sun leaves the sign of Capricorn. The thirty subtle channels of the elbow are associated with the lunar days of the month of Caitra (nag pa'i zla), during which the sun leaves the sign of Pisces. And so on for the rest of the main joints.    
The equivalent major joints of the left arm are associated with Aquarius, Aries and Gemini. Moreover, the three different rows of knuckles of the left hand are the same signs as are associated with the main joints of the left arm: the first, inner row of knuckles with Aquarius, the middle row with Aries and the outer row with Gemini. The equivalent associations for the right hand are Capricorn, Pisces and Taurus.    
From the three major joints of the left leg, the hip is associated with Leo, the knee with Libra and the ankle with Sagittarius. These same signs are associated with the rows of toe knuckles of the left foot. The equivalent joints of the right leg and toes are associated respectively with Cancer, Virgo and Scorpio. In the painting the subtle channels following the length of the arms and legs are depicted of different colors, indicating their association with the five elements of earth, water, fire, wind, and space, plus primordial wisdom.    
The thirty subtle channels of the right shoulder are associated with the thirty lunars days of the month of Māgha (mchu'i zla), during which the Sun leaves the sign of Capricorn. The thirty subtle channels of the elbow are associated with the lunar days of the month of Caitra (nag pa'i zla), during which the sun leaves the sign of Pisces. And so on for the rest of the main joints.    
Important Note:    
In The Inner Kalacakratantra: A Buddhist Tantric View of the Individual Vesna A. Wallace stresses, “The fact that the conversion of heterodox groups was one of the motivations behind the Kālacakratantra's adoption of specific non-Buddhist ideas implies that its teachings pertaining to the Kālacakra worldview were not kept secret from the public; that is, they were not guarded as secret teachings intended for an initiated elite. Moreover, the Kālacakra tradition's preference for explicitly presenting its specific tantric views is a result of its openly professed conversionary endeavors… The Buddhist tantric conversionary efforts are justified by the tantra as a skillful means for leading individuals of diverse mental dispositions to spiritual maturation.”    
Moreover, the tantra also warns that one should not grasp at one's own view in a dogmatic way, but one should be careful not to fall under the influence of other teachings “by familiarizing one-self with those teachings in order to refute them.”

Two Paintings from a Set of Eight Paintings:    
The images from a set of eight paintings that depict the outer and inner aspects of Kālacakra are said to have been painted under the direction of the Eighth Karmapa, Mikyö Dorje (mi bskyod rdo rje, 1507-1554); they were originally provided by Rossi and Rossi Ltd., of London, UK, and due to the Rubin Museum of Art, in New York, were accessible in higher resolution in 2010. Those higher resolution master images have been used for reworking them by Edward Henning. These paintings are also featured in the book by Martin Brauen, "Mandala, Sacred Circle in Tibetan Buddhism", published by Arnoldsche Art Publishers and the Rubin Museum of Art.
