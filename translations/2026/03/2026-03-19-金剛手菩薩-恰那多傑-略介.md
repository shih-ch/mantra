金剛手菩薩（Vajrapāṇi／藏語：恰那多傑〔Chana Dorje，待考〕）@nepalsbeststatues

Vajrapani or Chana Dorje is the protector and guide of the Buddha, one of the eight great Bodhisattva and lords of the three families.  
金剛手（Vajrapāṇi），亦稱恰那多傑，是佛陀的護持者與引導者，為八大菩薩之一，亦為三大族姓之主尊之一。

He represents the power of the Buddhas and is usually depicted as blue in color and holding a vajra.  
他代表諸佛的威神力，相貌多作青藍色身，手持金剛杵（vajra）。

Vajrapani is indeed a Bodhisattva who embodies all the Buddhas’ infinite enlightened power.  
金剛手確是一位菩薩，總攝並顯現一切諸佛無量無邊的覺悟威力。

In recognition of Vajrapani’s powerful abilities, Shakyamuni Buddha entrusted him with the protection of the tantras, those powerful Vajrayana teaching capable of guiding qualified disciples to full enlightenment in one short lifetime.  
由於金剛手具足廣大威能，釋迦牟尼佛遂將密續（tantra）託付於他護持；這些有力的金剛乘教法，能夠在僅僅一生之中，指引具足相應根器的弟子圓滿成佛。

As protector of these precious and esoteric tantric teachings, Vajrapani is sometimes referred to with the title “Lord of the Secret”.  
身為此等珍貴而深奧密續教法的護持者，金剛手有時也被尊稱為「秘密主」。

Vajrapani is the Tantric aspect of the enlightened mind, which transforms the energy of hate into active wisdom and magical perfection.  
金剛手是覺悟心之密續相，能將瞋恨之力轉化為積極行動的智慧與神妙圓滿。

A Bodhisattva-aspect which symbolizes the indestructible vajra-body of a Buddha.  
此一菩薩形相，象徵佛之不壞金剛身。

Chana Dorje was a major secret meditation deity of e.g. Lama Yeshe, Geshe Rabten and Gangchen Tulku.  
恰那多傑曾是如喇嘛耶喜、格西拉滇與甘青仁波切等人重要的秘密本尊。

Vajrapani is extensively represented in Buddhist iconography as one of the three protective deities surrounding the Buddha.  
在佛教形像中，金剛手經常被描繪為環繞佛陀的三位護法本尊之一。

Each of them symbolizes one of the Buddha’s virtues: Manjusri (the manifestation of all the Buddhas’ wisdom), Avalokitesvara (the manifestation of all the Buddhas’ compassion) and Vajrapani (the manifestation of all the Buddhas’ power as well as the power of all 5 Tathagathas).  
此三位各自象徵佛陀的一種功德：文殊師利（Manjuśrī）為一切諸佛智慧之顯現，觀世音（Avalokiteśvara）為一切諸佛大悲之顯現，而金剛手（Vajrapāṇi）則是一切諸佛威力，以及五方如來一切威德之顯現。

Vajrapani uses powerful means to protect and promote Buddha’s teaching.  
金剛手以種種大威力之方便，護持並弘揚佛陀教法。

On one famous occasion, Shakyamuni Buddha was seated at the Vulture’s Peak near Rajagriha, the site where He delivered the Perfection of Wisdom Sutra.  
有一次著名的事例是：釋迦牟尼佛在王舍城附近的靈鷲山安坐說法，即彼時宣說《般若波羅蜜多經》之處。

At that time, Buddha’s jealous cousin Devadatta rolled a large boulder down the hill in an attempt to assassinate Him.  
當時，佛陀心懷嫉妒的堂兄提婆達多，從山上推下一塊巨石，企圖刺殺佛陀。

Just as the huge rock was about to crush Buddha, Vajrapani used his immense power to split it into two so that pieces of the boulder fell harmlessly to either side.  
就在那塊巨石即將壓向佛陀之際，金剛手以其廣大威力將巨石劈成兩半，使其碎塊分落兩側，並未傷及佛陀。
